# Credits

## Plant images

All 16 plant cut-outs in `assets/plants/` are original, AI-generated
bird's-eye studio shots made for florest on a flat magenta backdrop, then cut
out, despilled, trimmed and exported as 768 px WebP by `tool/key_magenta.py`.
No third-party photos are bundled.
